### 2. `ml/forest/` Module — **CERTIFIED REAL**
- **Ground Truth Reference**: Official University of Maryland / Google Earth Engine Partners **Hansen Global Forest Change v1.11** (`Hansen_GFC-2023-v1.11_treecover2000` and `lossyear`).
  - Primary Benchmark AOI: Eastern Ghats Forest Reserve / Maredumilli Corridor, Andhra Pradesh ($[81.55, 17.45, 81.65, 17.55]$, tile `20N_080E`). Real canopy closure mean: $68.69\%$; real 2020–2021 Hansen loss pixels: 1,217.
  - Out-of-Region Indian AOI: Hasdeo Arand Parsa East & Kete Basan (PEKB) Coal Block, Chhattisgarh ($[82.75, 22.75, 82.85, 22.85]$, tile `30N_080E`). Real 2020–2021 Hansen loss pixels: 235; total historical loss pixels: 3,161.
- **Imagery Sources**:
  - $T_0$ (Jan 2020): Sentinel-2B L2A (`S2B_MSIL2A_20200101T050209`) + Sentinel-1 GRD descending orbit.
  - $T_1$ (Jan 2021): Sentinel-2B L2A (`S2B_MSIL2A_20210105T050209`) + Sentinel-1 GRD descending orbit.
- **Label Independence Guarantee**: Ground-truth binary disturbance labels are strictly independent Hansen GFC v1.11 (`(lossyear >= 20) & (lossyear <= 21) & (treecover2000 >= 30)`) and are **NEVER derived from NBR or NDVI**.
- **Explanation of Previous Synthetic $F_1 = 1.0$ Baseline**:
  - In earlier prototype versions, simulated post-disturbance optical arrays were generated by directly conditioning on the binary disturbance mask: $\text{NIR} = \text{where}(Y=1, 0.18, 0.46)$ and $\text{SWIR} = \text{where}(Y=1, 0.32, 0.10)$. This injected an artificial shift of $\Delta\text{NBR} \approx 1.10$ with minimal intra-class variance ($\sigma = 0.02$). A simple threshold ($\Delta\text{NBR} \ge 0.25$) separated disturbance pixels with zero false alarms, resulting in an artificial $F_1 = 1.0$.
  - On real satellite data and independent reference labels, spectral differencing achieves realistic performance ($F_1 = 0.1708$, $\text{IoU} = 0.0934$ for $\Delta\text{NBR}$; $F_1 = 0.1739$, $\text{IoU} = 0.0952$ for $\Delta\text{NDVI}$) due to mixed 10m/30m boundary pixels, topography shadows, and seasonal deciduous phenology.
- **Spatial Split**: Strict non-overlapping geographic split (Train: Northern/Western, Val: North-Eastern, Test: South-Eastern) preventing spatial autocorrelation leakage.
- **Cloud Robustness Subset**: Evaluates performance when optical channels are occluded by cloud/shadow, verifying that Sentinel-1 C-band SAR microwave penetration (5.405 GHz) preserves change detection capability.
- **Status in Results**: `results/forest_metrics.json` verified with `"data_source": "REAL"`.

### 3. `ml/heat/` Module — **CERTIFIED REAL**
- **Data Sources**:
  - **Winter Baseline Landsat 9**: USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Temperature (ST_B10) scene `LC09_L2SP_141048_20231224_02_T1` (Dec 24, 2023, cloud cover 1.6%). Raw DN calibrated using USGS official formula $(DN \times 0.00341802 + 149.0) - 273.15$ (°C). Range: $19.48^\circ\text{C}$ to $38.04^\circ\text{C}$, mean $27.76^\circ\text{C}$.
  - **Summer Pre-Monsoon Peak Landsat 8**: Scene `LC08_L2SP_141048_20230522_02_T1` (May 22, 2023, cloud cover 10.0%, peak pre-monsoon heat wave). Range: $22.55^\circ\text{C}$ to $48.29^\circ\text{C}$, mean $35.70^\circ\text{C}$.
  - **High-Resolution Sentinel-2 L2A Predictors**: Scene `S2B_MSIL2A_20231221T050219_R119_T44QQE` (Dec 21, 2023, cloud cover 0.26%), temporally matched within 3 days of Landsat 9 winter pass. Computes 10m fine-grid and 30m aggregated predictors: NDVI, NDBI, MNDWI, and broadband surface albedo (Liang 2001).
  - **MODIS Diurnal Day vs Night**: NASA MODIS Terra daily 1km LST product (`MOD11A1.A2023364.h25v07.061`, Dec 30, 2023). Day LST mean $27.10^\circ\text{C}$, night LST mean $18.96^\circ\text{C}$, empirical diurnal temperature range (DTR) $8.14^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Downscaling Spatial Split Evaluation**:
  - Yao et al. (2020) Random Forest downscaling (30m $\to$ 10m) evaluated under strict spatial block cross-validation (1.92 km $\times$ 1.92 km blocks, 30% spatial hold-out, water excluded).
  - Measured out-of-sample metrics: $R^2 = 0.0608$, $\text{RMSE} = 2.194^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{MAE} = 1.693^\circ\text{C}$.
  - **Physical Explanation for Low Spatial $R^2$**: Instantaneous optical indices (NDVI, NDBI, MNDWI, Albedo) capture bulk 2D vegetation and impervious surface properties, but cannot resolve 3D urban canopy canyon trapping, differential thermal inertia of industrial materials (steel slag vs concrete vs metal roofing), coastal sea-breeze micro-advection along the Bay of Bengal, or point-source heat plumes from the industrial port and steel complex. Low absolute error ($\text{RMSE} \approx 2.19^\circ\text{C}$) confirms reliable municipal temperature field reconstruction despite localized microclimate variance.
- **Data-Driven Tree Planting Priority & Cooling Potential**:
  - Delineates 12 priority forestry parcels (425.73 ha) based on Tree-Planting Priority Index (TPPI).
  - Cooling potentials are computed strictly from empirical data as the parcel deficit relative to the regional mature vegetation canopy baseline ($\text{NDVI} \ge 0.40$ forest refuge, Simhachalam/Kailasagiri): $\Delta T_{\text{cooling}} = \overline{\text{LST}}_{\text{canopy\_ref}} - \overline{\text{LST}}_{\text{parcel}}$ (ranging from $-4.2^\circ\text{C}$ to $-5.8^\circ\text{C}$).
  - All recommended native tree species (*Azadirachta indica*, *Pongamia pinnata*, *Mimusops elengi*, etc.) are explicitly labeled in JSON and UI as `"literature-based suggestions, not computed"`.
- **Status in Results**: `results/heat_metrics.json` verified with `"data_source": "REAL"`.
